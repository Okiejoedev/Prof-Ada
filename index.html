For the morning of the exam

A prayer to pray before you walk in

Father, thank You for bringing me this far. You gave me this place and this work, and You have been with me in every late night.

Calm my heart. Clear my mind. Bring back to my memory everything I have studied, and give me understanding beyond my own. Let me read carefully, think clearly, and write with confidence.

Whatever the result, I trust You with it, because You began this good work in me and You will finish it. In Jesus' name.

Amen.

You're tired +

Close the book for ten minutes, Ada. Drink water, eat something real, and breathe. Rest is part of the work. Even God rested on the seventh day.

"Come unto me, all ye that labour and are heavy laden, and I will give you rest." Matthew 11:28

I'm proud of how hard you've been going.

You doubt yourself +

You did not get into a Chemistry PhD by accident. Every late night in that lab has been building something in you. You know more than you think you do.

Read each question twice. Start with the one you know best. Trust what you studied.

God shall help her, and that right early. Psalm 46:5

Look at the clocks at the top of this page. Whatever time it says in Lagos, I'm thinking about you, and most likely praying for you.

Call me when you're free. Any hour.
